After the death of his parents and sister, introverted high school student Cha
Hyun-su is left completely alone, and he has to move into a small apartment in a
rundown residential complex Green Home. Seeing no reason to live on, the guy
decides to commit suicide, but suddenly some people start turning into
aggressive monsters. Hyun-su will have to overcome himself and get to know his
new neighbors better in order to join forces and confront the unexpected threat.
